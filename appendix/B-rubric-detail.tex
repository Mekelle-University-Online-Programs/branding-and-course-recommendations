\chapter{QA Rubric Detail}

For each of the 14 categories, scoring bands:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{5 — Exemplary:} exceeds expectation; no remediation required.
  \item \textbf{4 — Proficient:} meets expectation; minor optional improvements only.
  \item \textbf{3 — Developing:} meets minimum; specific remediation items identified.
  \item \textbf{2 — Beginning:} does not meet minimum; substantial rework required.
  \item \textbf{1 — Not present:} category not addressed.
  \item \textbf{0 — Not applicable:} explicitly justified.
\end{itemize}

Pass threshold: \textbf{weighted average $\geq 4.0$}, and no category below 3.
